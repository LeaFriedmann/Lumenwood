LUMENWOOD

Please rotate your device to play.

How to play:

Collect acorns throughout the level.
You can throw acorns at mushrooms to defeat them. Small mushrooms can also be defeated by jumping on them.
Be careful not to waste all your acorns — you will need them later to defeat the final boss. The boss can only be defeated by throwing acorns at him.

Controls:

Jump

Left

Right

Throw
