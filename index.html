LUMENWOOD

Please rotate your device to play.

How to play:

Collect bottles throughout the level.
You can throw bottles at chickens to defeat them. Chickens can also be defeated by jumping on them.
Be careful not to waste all your bottles — you will need them later to defeat the final boss. The boss can only be defeated by throwing bottles at him.

Controls:

Arrow Up: Jump
Arrow Left: Move left
Arrow Right: Move right
Press D to throw bottles.
